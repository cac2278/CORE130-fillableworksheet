CORE 130 · AFTER YOU SEARCH

Reflect on what became visible.

Many of the systems we use to organize and find information developed within scholarly traditions shaped by patriarchy. Women’s and Gender Studies knowledge can therefore be harder to find using the most obvious terms and pathways. WGS research often requires us to search creatively—and then reflect on what our search made visible, what it missed, and why.

  1. What became visible through your lens that another discipline might have missed?
  2. Even though our question considers gender, race, and socioeconomic background, whose experiences could still be missing from the research? Why is this important?
  3. What can your chosen source help us understand about this question? What can’t it tell us? What other evidence could we use to understand what’s missing?
  4. How did your choices—discipline, keywords, and database—shape what you were able to find? What might have changed if you had searched differently?
  5. What stayed with you from this experience? Why?
Look across your answers: what did your research process make easier to see, and what remains harder to see?
